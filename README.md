## The four kinds of Best Buy discounts that actually exist

Worth separating these, because people lump them all under "coupon" and then wonder why a code from a random site did nothing.

**Open-box and clearance pricing.** Best Buy's open-box items are discounted by condition (excellent, good, fair), and clearance and open-box stock is regularly cut by up to 50%. No code needed. The discount is already in the price, which also means it survives everything else you stack.

**Deal of the Day, flash sales, and sale events.** Best Buy rotates 24-hour discounts on laptops, TVs, headphones and appliances, and runs larger events around Presidents Day, Memorial Day, back-to-school, July, Black Friday and Cyber Monday. Coupon supply tends to spike around those windows too, so codes that stopped working in February often resurface later in the year with a different value.

**Brand-funded promotions.** A big share of what gets advertised as a Best Buy deal is a manufacturer offer routed through Best Buy: save up to $500 on select Windows PCs, up to $400 on AMD Ryzen laptops, up to $1,000 on select LG appliances, up to $120 on Dyson vacuums, gift cards bundled with Samsung or LG purchases. These are the closest thing to real "coupons" and they're usually listed on Best Buy's own deals pages, not on coupon aggregators.

**Membership and card rewards.** The free My Best Buy program earns 1% back on eligible purchases (0.5 points per $1), and every 250 points converts to a $5 Best Buy coupon. The My Best Buy credit card earns 5% back in rewards on Best Buy purchases, with a 10%-back first-purchase offer. Financing runs 6 to 24 months depending on category, and it's deferred interest, which means missing the payoff date by a dollar can retroactively charge you interest on the full original amount.

One thing you can stop looking for: a permanent storewide 20%-off-first-order Best Buy code. Multiple coupon desks have checked, and it doesn't exist as a standing offer. If a site claims otherwise, read the terms before you build a $900 order around it.

## The stacking mistake that costs more than the coupon saves

Here's the mechanics that trip people up. Cashback is paid by retailers through affiliate commissions, and the commission is attributed by a tracking cookie from the click. Only one source can claim that click.

So if you click through a cashback platform to Best Buy, then let a coupon browser extension from another site fire a code at checkout, that other extension can overwrite the tracking. The code may well apply, and the cashback may well vanish. ShopBack's own guidance says exactly this: codes listed on the merchant's page inside the platform are pre-vetted to stay compatible with cashback, and third-party coupon aggregators can overwrite tracking.

Best Buy adds more rules on top. Purchases made with coupon or discount codes that ShopBack hasn't authorised can be excluded from cashback. So can orders paid with gift cards, and orders flagged as reseller or purchasing-agent transactions.

The practical sequence looks like this:

- Start your shopping session on the cashback platform, never on BestBuy.com.
- Use the codes listed on that merchant page, not codes scraped from a coupon site.
- Stay in the same session and on the same device from click to checkout. Don't switch from desktop to app mid-order.
- Don't run two coupon extensions or two cashback extensions at once.
- Pay with a rewards card so the card layer lands on top of the discounted total.
- Skip ad blockers during checkout if you're in a market where that interferes with tracking.

Done in that order, you're getting three discounts funded by three different parties: the manufacturer's promotion, the merchant's own sale price, and the commission share returned to you.

## Which Best Buy purchases don't earn cashback

This is the part deal pages rarely spell out, and it's the biggest source of "my cashback never showed up" complaints.

Across Best Buy campaigns, these are consistently excluded:

- Apple hardware: iPad, MacBook, desktop and laptop Apple products, iPhone hardware, Apple Watches
- Big-ticket categories in many campaigns: video game consoles and handhelds, desktop and gaming PCs, laptops, graphics cards and computer components
- Gift cards, warranties, subscriptions, services, mobile plans and digital downloads
- Refurbished products, and third-party Marketplace sellers in several categories
- Orders paid with gift cards or with promo codes the platform hasn't authorised
- Cancelled, returned, exchanged or price-adjusted orders, including partial returns
- Orders flagged as reseller, purchasing agent or bulk purchases

Categories that typically do qualify: standard TVs, small kitchen appliances, computer accessories, non-Beats audio, action cameras, physical games and personal care. And campaign rules override the general list, so a category excluded last week may be included this week.

Best Buy's coupon desk can only research missing rewards for 30 days past the order date, so if something doesn't track, file the claim quickly with the order number, date and subtotal.
